Harvest Table: A Community Thanksgiving Dinner

Pull up a chair with neighbors for a warm holiday meal supporting local families. 100% of tickets and donations go to the Harvest Hands Food Bank of Monmouth.

When Saturday, November 28, 2026 3:00 – 7:00 PM
Where Marlboro Recreation Center 1996 Recreation Way, Marlboro, NJ 07746

Every Seat Fills a Local Pantry

100% of tickets and donations go to the Harvest Hands Food Bank of Monmouth to stock shelves and provide holiday dinners across Monmouth County.
